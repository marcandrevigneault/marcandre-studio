Cape Forchu, Nova Scotia · November 2025
Between Tides
A grey coast between the tides — fog on the water, salt on the lens, and the long patience of slow weather.

After the warm grey of Kyoto and the white glare of the salt, the coast offers a third kind of light — cold, slow, and patient. Here the tide does the composing, and the camera waits for the fog to decide what it will let you keep.

The cape disappears, an hour at a time.

The light works harder than you think — steady in the mist while everything else goes soft.
